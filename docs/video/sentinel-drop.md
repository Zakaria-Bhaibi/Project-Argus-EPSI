# « Sentinel Drop »: 60 s teaser, Veo 3 prompt pack

Format required by the brief: vertical **9:16**, ≤ 60 s, MP4 H.264, file `Workshop2026-M1-G<n>-VidDrop.mp4`.

## How the 60 s are built
- 6 slots of 10 s. Each slot = **one Veo 3 clip (8 s, Veo's maximum)** + **2 s of real project footage** (screen recording or green-screen shot) cut in CapCut.
- **Real screens are never generated by AI:** Veo garbles text and UI. To show the dashboard *inside* a cinematic shot, the prompt asks for a monitor showing a **solid chroma-green screen**. In CapCut, key that green out (Chroma key) and put your real screen recording underneath.
- **The brief makes two parts human:** the team in front of the green screen (30–50 s) and the team facing the camera (50–60 s). Veo makes the *backgrounds* for those slots; you film yourselves in the green-screen room and key yourselves on top.
- **Voice-over** is recorded by a team member or with ElevenLabs, in French, and added in CapCut. The Veo prompts ask for **sound effects only, no dialogue**.

## In Flow (Veo 3)
New project → **Frames to Video** when a first image is given, **Text to Video** otherwise. Aspect ratio **9:16**, mode **Fast** (cheaper, almost the same quality), then upscale to 1080p before downloading.

## Style block: paste it at the end of EVERY prompt (never edit it)
```
Style: cinematic photoreal, vertical 9:16 framing, night on a remote windswept plateau, cold blue moonlight with
teal rim light, deep navy shadows, accents of emerald green and amber warning light, anamorphic lens, shallow depth
of field, subtle film grain, slow deliberate camera, no on-screen text unless specified, no dialogue, natural sound
effects only.
```

## Editing (CapCut)
- **Cuts on the beat:** cut on each siren, each impact, each glow. No slow transitions in a 60-second clip.
- **Sound:** keep Veo's sound effects, lower them under the voice-over (−12 dB), and add a light electro music track.
- **Captions:** big French subtitles in the middle third (the brief targets the Reels/TikTok format), sentence case.
- **Export:** 1080×1920, H.264, 30 fps, ≤ 60.0 s → `Workshop2026-M1-G<n>-VidDrop.mp4`.

## If a clip comes out badly
- **Messed-up text in slot 2 or 6:** regenerate (Veo handles text well, but not every time) or add the text in CapCut.
- **Green screen not uniform in slot 3:** say "matte flat solid green, no glare" and regenerate, or crop to the monitor in CapCut.
- **Inconsistent style between clips:** check that the style block is pasted word for word.
